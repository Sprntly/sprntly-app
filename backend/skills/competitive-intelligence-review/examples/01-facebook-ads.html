Where Facebook Ads Stands

The automation race is over and everyone finished. Every platform we compete with now ships fully automated buying with AI creative attached — Reddit in January, Google's Gemini rebuild in May, TikTok's agent in June. What was our structural advantage two years ago is the category's entry ticket. Two competitors are now attacking the same seam, independently, and it is ours: Reddit sells automation you can see into and grew ad revenue 74%; Snap sells onboarding and support and has nearly tripled SMB advertisers. Neither can beat us on reach, so neither is trying.
But the thing that could actually take the business is not in the feature set. Product discovery is moving upstream into AI assistants — AI platforms are projected to carry $20.9B of retail spend this year, nearly four times last year — and we own no surface in that journey. OpenAI built a working ad platform in five months, partly with our people. That is the section to read first.

Against the scale players
Against the specialists

Read the left chart first. Our shape and Google's are nearly the same shape — that is the definition of a commoditised contest, and they are ahead of us on intent, which is structural and uncopyable. TikTok extends past both of us on two axes: creative tooling and trust. Their trust score is not a judgment call about brand — it is a shipped feature, applied automatically to every asset they generate.

The right chart is the uncomfortable one. Reddit is a company at roughly 1% of our ad revenue, and it extends past us on four of eight dimensions — automation transparency, measurement proof, advertiser experience and intent signal. OpenAI, five months into having a product at all, already extends past us on intent and commerce proximity. Neither of them is trying to be us. They have each found an axis where scale is irrelevant and taken it.

Where all shapes overlap is commodity. Where a rival extends past us is their sales pitch. And the only axis where we extend past everyone is reach — which is the one attribute a challenger never has to beat.

The one that matters

Five of seven threats have no named defence. That is not a scoring artefact — it reflects that our roadmap is organised around competing inside the feed while the contested ground has moved to what happens before anyone reaches one.

The timing is better than it looks. OpenAI pulled back from in-ChatGPT checkout in March after discovering that fulfilment, payments, tax compliance and fraud are genuinely hard S. Agentic commerce is arriving slower than its advocates promised. That buys us a window — it does not remove the threat, and the window is the only reason recommendation 5 is a research brief rather than an emergency.

What to do
Five, drawing on every section above. Ranked by leverage.
01 · Product
Make Advantage+ delivery legible
FromRadar (transparency, worst gap) · Reddit launch log (Top Audience Personas is their only net-new and their whole strategy) · sentiment (opacity, mapped to Reddit) · threat scan (transparency becoming category standard, no defence)
BuildA per-campaign delivery explanation in Ads Manager and mobile: top three drivers of budget allocation this period in plain language; audience clusters receiving delivery, labelled readably rather than as opaque IDs; creative-level share of delivery with the reason each asset won or lost; a timestamped change log of automated decisions, so a performance drop has a visible cause.
Why nowTwo competitors validated the demand independently with their own roadmaps, and Reddit proved transparency and automation are not a trade-off — they made the automated campaign more legible than the manual one.
MeasureSupport contacts tagged to unexplained performance change · Advantage+ retention after a delivery drop · movement in the opacity theme by month three.
WatchExplanations that expose auction mechanics. Scope to advertiser-actionable causes only.
02 · Product + Policy
Provenance by default on every generated asset
FromRadar (trust — TikTok extends furthest past us) · TikTok launch log (applied automatically, net-new) · threat scan (disclosure regulation, no defence)
BuildC2PA credentials, invisible watermark and a platform-applied label attached at generation rather than publish, surviving duplication, editing and delivery reporting. Remove the advertiser-facing toggle — provenance becomes a property of the asset, not a setting.
Why nowConsumer distrust of heavy brand AI use doubled from 20% to 40% in a year. TikTok already complies on day one of any mandate; we would run a migration.
MeasureShare of generated assets carrying credentials · generation-to-publish rate, which is the real risk.
03 · Product
Brand guardrails — make generated creative safe for advertisers who won't risk their brand
FromSentiment (AI creative quality, mapped to TikTok and Google) · Google launch log (brief-based generation claims the brand workflow) · radar (creative tooling)
BuildThree primitives. Brand kit lock — generation bounded by uploaded logo, palette and type, violations block rather than warn. Claim allowlist — the model may not assert a product attribute absent from the supplied catalog or brand profile. Hero-creative gate — designated assets that automation may not alter under any enhancement.
Why nowAdoption is not the problem — over 4M advertisers use the generative tools. Usable adoption is. Every generation feature shipped without this widens the gap between advertisers who tried the tools and advertisers who trust them with a launch.
MeasureShare of generated assets published without edit · large-advertiser opt-in to Advantage+ creative.
04 · Product · Cheapest win here
Fix mobile Ads Manager reliability and the support floor
FromSentiment (reliability and support — the only themes with no competitive beneficiary) · radar (advertiser experience, where both Reddit and Snap extend past us)
BuildPublish-state integrity as a tracked reliability SLO with alerting; a mobile-web parity audit on every campaign-editing surface, with web-only fields explicitly labelled in-app rather than silently missing; a restriction path that names the specific triggering policy instead of a generic category.
Why nowNothing here costs strategy, and it is visible to every advertiser who opens the app. Snap is winning SMB on exactly this ground and says so publicly.
MeasureApp-store rating and review sentiment, tracked monthly here · publish-failure rate · support contacts per 1,000 active advertisers.
05 · Strategy · Largest exposure
Build a position on discovery moving upstream
FromThreat scan (three of the top four threats, all with no named defence) · Google launch log (NRF commerce suite) · OpenAI launch log (checkout retired — the delay that buys the window) · radar (commerce proximity, where we trail four of five)
BuildNot an assistant. Three things: a consideration-stage signal strategy — what can we observe about pre-purchase research on our own surfaces that we currently discard; a position on commerce protocols before one standardises without us; and a measurement standard for AI-surface advertising, which is available precisely because OpenAI's analytics are immature today.
Why nowAI platforms are projected to carry $20.9B of retail spend this year against roughly $5B last. Agentic checkout slipped in March, which is why this is a research and standards brief rather than an emergency — but the window closes when someone else defines the standard.
EscalateThe commercial half of this is not a product problem. Agency relationships being rebuilt inside a competitor by the person who owned them here belongs with leadership, not a roadmap.
