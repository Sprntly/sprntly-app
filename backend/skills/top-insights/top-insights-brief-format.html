Reports decompose
A monthly review isn't one insight — it's six, released across the month.
The instinct is to give a report its own standing item on the brief. That's the trap: it's the stalest possible object, unchanging by construction for its whole interval. Instead the strongest finding cards the cycle the report lands and carries the report link, and the rest surface one at a time on their own merits, each linking back to the same parent.
per_cycle = ceil( findings ÷ (refresh_days ÷ brief_days) ), floor 1, cap 2
6 findings, monthly report, daily brief → ~1 every 5 days
6 findings, monthly report, weekly brief → 1–2 per week
high urgency bypasses pacing entirely

The report shelf
The one place a report is allowed to sit still.
Decomposition governs cards. The shelf is navigation, and it works precisely because it isn't a card — a reader wondering "wasn't there something about pricing in the competitive review?" can get there from any brief, on any day of the month, without the brief re-surfacing the finding.
  • One line, with the report's own takeaway. A bare link says a report exists; it doesn't say whether to bother. The sentence comes verbatim from the report — this skill never writes it.
  • Every row shows when it ran. Stating the age is what makes a month-old link acceptable in a daily digest.
  • Below the cards, always. Never competes for ranking, never counts toward the card budget or the greeting total.
  • Subscribed and available only. An empty shelf renders nothing rather than a placeholder.

Anti-staleness
A card slot must be earned by news.
Every finding is compared against a ledger of what was shown before and assigned one state. Only two of them earn a card — anything unchanged stays ranked on the backlog rather than repeating.
new — never surfaced beforeCard
updated — surfaced before and materially changedCard
carried — surfaced before, nothing changedBacklog
in progress — PRD approved or patch mergedBacklog
resolved — fixed, shipped, or recoveredCelebrate
  • Material change is numeric — pain ±15%, reach ±25%, urgency tier, value ±20%, or status. A newly-added channel counts too: interviews landing mid-month move no number, but the reader genuinely knows more.
  • Rotation exhaustion retires anything shown three times with no action and no change. It returns only if it worsens.
  • Resolution advances the queue — acting on a card promotes the next backlog item in.

The reframe
Report the finding — don't prescribe the fix.
The old voice led with "we've drafted the PRD, review and approve it," which skips the step where the PM decides whether the problem is real. Readers experience that as being sold to. Three changes carry the new posture:
  • The title sizes the stake, not the reward. Not "fixing them protects $4.1M" but "$4.1M in renewals sit with the accounts hitting them."
  • The body's third beat is the evidence base — what the claim rests on, stated plainly. The CTA does the inviting.
  • Evidence takes the primary CTA, or the parent report where one exists. The PRD moves to the ghost slot and reads Generate PRD until one exists.
